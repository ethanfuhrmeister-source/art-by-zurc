Privacy

Zurc Catalog website and iPhone app · updated November 13, 2026

Zurc Catalog shows Rocio's handmade pottery and paintings. You can browse the catalogue on this website or in the Zurc Catalog app for iPhone without an account.

What the app collects

The app collects nothing about you. It has no accounts for shoppers, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. It downloads the catalogue and its photos from zurccatalog.com and keeps a copy on your phone so it opens without signal.

The studio sign-in

The artist signs in with a studio password to edit the catalogue. That sign-in is kept only on her phone. Photos she takes or picks in the app are uploaded to this website to show her pieces. The app sees only the photos she picks.

Buying a piece

Purchases are handled by Square or Stripe, never inside the app. Their own privacy policies cover what you give them when you pay.

The website

If you join the show list in the footer, your email address is used only to tell you about upcoming shows. Ask to be removed at any time.
